# Why this rebuild

Chosen 2 November 2026 as the first rebuild in the pharma and medtech field CRM family.

- **The bill.** Veeva publishes no price for Veeva CRM or Vault CRM: it is sold per named user per year on a private quote, usually with an implementation partner. Every Veeva CRM customer has to move: Veeva ends support for Veeva CRM on the Salesforce platform on 31 December 2029 and moves customers to Vault CRM (Veeva investor news and the FY2026 annual report, checked 2 November 2026). A forced re-implementation is the moment a company asks what it pays for.
- **Data-shaped.** Territories, reps, products, practices, healthcare professionals, calls, starter pack requests and deliveries, lots and rep stock, events, attendees and transfers of value, with weekly rituals: the call plan, coverage against target, starter packs out, stock counts, recalls, events and the half-yearly transparency report.
- **The rules are real and checkable.** Medicines Australia Code of Conduct Edition 20 ss 4.5, 6.1 and 14; Medicines New Zealand Code of Practice Edition 17 ss 4.8 and 4.18; the Code Tool Kit hospitality cap. They turn into refusals, dates and a report file.
- **Who pays for it.** New Zealand and Australian subsidiaries of medicines companies, generics and over-the-counter companies, and medtech distributors with 5 to 60 reps.
- **What stays with the custom build.** A phone app for reps in the car, approved email and presentation content, the Disclosure Australia upload itself, the State and Territory sampling rules, master data feeds of professionals, and alignment tools for redrawing territories.
